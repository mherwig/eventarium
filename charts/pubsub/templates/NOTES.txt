defaults for the JWT settings and panics without them, so pass the same
values the services got. kubectl run spells it --env - there is no -e:

  # there, .Values.config is not what the pods got

    --env=JWT_SECRET=<from your secret> --env=JWT_ISSUER=<from your secret> --env=JWT_AUDIENCE=<from your secret> -- \
{{- else }}

Verify the install:

  helm test {{ .Release.Name }}

Notes
- sse-gateway sets NODE_ID to the pod name, so every pod owns its own
  `sse-live-<NODE_ID>` group. Rollouts therefore leave idle groups behind;
  Kafka drops them after offsets retention (7 days by default).
- The stores are single instances by design (see charts/pubsub-infra): the
  eight services tolerate losing a node, the databases do not.
