pubsub-infra installed: three each of Kafka, PostgreSQL and ClickHouse.

  Kafka       {{ include "pubsub-infra.kafkaService" . }}:9092   (3 KRaft nodes, RF=3)
  PostgreSQL  {{ include "pubsub-infra.postgresWriteService" . }}:5432  (primary)
              {{ include "pubsub-infra.postgresService" . }}-ro:5432  (standbys, read-only)
              {{ include "pubsub-infra.postgresService" . }}-r:5432   (any instance)
  ClickHouse  {{ include "pubsub-infra.clickhouseService" . }}:8123 (HTTP) / :9000 (native)

Check they are up:

  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
  kubectl get cluster {{ include "pubsub-infra.postgresService" . }}      # instances, primary, readiness
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "pubsub-infra.postgresWriteService" . }} 5432:5432

PostgreSQL runs three instances on CloudNativePG and fails over on its own:
losing the primary's pod promotes a standby and moves the `-rw` Service to it,
so PG_DSN needs no change. Point clients at `-rw`, never at the bare cluster
name - CNPG owns that naming and it is not a Service.

Kafka and ClickHouse replicate the same way PostgreSQL does, by different
means: three KRaft voters at RF=3, and three ClickHouse nodes with
ReplicatedMergeTree over an embedded Keeper ensemble. All three stores lose a
node without losing data or write access. What none of them covers is losing
the *node they share* - see values.yaml.

The app chart needs CH_CLUSTER so its ClickHouse DDL reaches all three
(`ON CLUSTER`); it is set in charts/pubsub/config.

Next: install the app chart, which runs `admin all` (topics, migrations,
ClickHouse table) as a hook before the services start.

  helm upgrade --install pubsub charts/pubsub \
    --set config.KAFKA_BROKERS={{ include "pubsub-infra.kafkaService" . }}:9092 \
    --set config.PG_DSN=postgres://{{ .Values.postgres.username }}:PASSWORD@{{ include "pubsub-infra.postgresWriteService" . }}:5432/{{ .Values.postgres.database }}?sslmode=disable \
    --set config.CH_DSN=clickhouse://{{ .Values.clickhouse.username }}:PASSWORD@{{ include "pubsub-infra.clickhouseService" . }}:9000/{{ .Values.clickhouse.database }}
