pubsub-infra installed: one Kafka, one PostgreSQL, one ClickHouse.

  Kafka       {{ include "pubsub-infra.kafkaService" . }}:9092   (KRaft, single node)
  PostgreSQL  {{ include "pubsub-infra.postgresService" . }}:5432
  ClickHouse  {{ include "pubsub-infra.clickhouseService" . }}:8123 (HTTP) / :9000 (native)

Check they are up:

  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "pubsub-infra.postgresService" . }} 5432:5432

These are single instances by design (values.yaml): the application layer runs
three replicas of every component, the stores do not - they are the remaining
single points of failure.

Next: install the app chart, which runs `admin all` (topics, migrations,
ClickHouse table) as a hook before the services start.

  helm upgrade --install pubsub charts/pubsub \
    --set config.KAFKA_BROKERS={{ include "pubsub-infra.kafkaService" . }}:9092 \
    --set config.PG_DSN=postgres://{{ .Values.postgres.username }}:PASSWORD@{{ include "pubsub-infra.postgresService" . }}:5432/{{ .Values.postgres.database }}?sslmode=disable \
    --set config.CH_DSN=clickhouse://{{ .Values.clickhouse.username }}:PASSWORD@{{ include "pubsub-infra.clickhouseService" . }}:9000/{{ .Values.clickhouse.database }}
